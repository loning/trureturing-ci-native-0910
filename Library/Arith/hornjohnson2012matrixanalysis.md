# Matrix Analysis

Second edition, Cambridge University Press.

Fischer's inequality compares a positive definite matrix with its principal
diagonal blocks. Taking one block of order two and all remaining blocks of
order one gives the determinant bound formalized here. The integer loss and
the logarithmic selector estimate are subsequent arithmetic deductions.
