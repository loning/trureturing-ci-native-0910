# OEIS A385590

The entry was authored on 2025-07-03. On 2026-09-08 the worker retrieved
the internal-format page and read its definition and formula field directly.
The formula field still says:

> Conjecture: Sum_{k=1..n} (-1)^k * binomial(n-1, k-1) * T(n, k) = 0
> for n > 2 and (-1)^n for n < 3.

The row index is the unique i > 1 with F(i) <= n < F(i+1), and
T(n,k) = F(i-1)^2 + 1 - ((i-1) mod 2) + (n-F(i))*F(i-2)
+ (k-1)*F(i-1). Here F(0)=0 and F(1)=1. Rows begin at n=1.
The separate comment that the triangle permutes the natural numbers is
not the statement addressed by this module.

The statement and triangle definition come from this entry. The proof is
derived in this repository by cancelling the constant and linear moments
of the alternating binomial coefficients. This note does not attribute
that proof to Schulte or OEIS.
