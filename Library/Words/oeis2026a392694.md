## Implementation route after search

Opened the linked Arndt site and its full fxtbook PDF (978 pages), extracted
its text, and inspected the A000009 passage at printed pages 348-350.
Equations 16.4-22 through 16.4-25 concern classical odd/distinct partitions;
they do not identify zero-prepended first sums. The target is therefore not
downgraded on this evidence. No first-publication priority is claimed.

Before implementation, the concrete representation of the preregistered
bridge is fixed as Ferrers conjugation: reverse s into decreasing row lengths,
transpose its Young diagram, and replace each column height h by 2*h-1.
A column of height k-i+1 occurs exactly t_i times, so this is the caller's
same odd-multiplicity map. Existing `YoungDiagram.transpose` and
`YoungDiagram.equivListRowLens` will supply conjugation; the new live obligation
is weight preservation for the first-sums map and the inverse correspondence.
Positivity in the row-length subtype forces the leading increment and excludes
padding with fictitious zero rows. The empty diagram handles n=0.
