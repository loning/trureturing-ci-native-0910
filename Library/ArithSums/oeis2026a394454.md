# OEIS A394454

The entry is "Sum of distinct prime divisors of C(2*n, n)". Its comment reads:

> Conjecture: for every prime p, the set {n >= 1 : p divides C(2*n, n)} is infinite.

The following comment reads:

> Consequently, every prime appears infinitely often in a(n), and lim sup a(n) = oo.

## Mathematical status

This conclusion is a direct corollary of Kummer's carry criterion. It is not
presented as a new theorem. Its value here is to account for the public
assertion recorded by OEIS, not to claim mathematical novelty or priority.

There is a still shorter binding argument: Catalan integrality gives
`n+1` dividing `C(2*n,n)` for every natural `n`. Infinitely many positive
indices satisfy `n = p-1` modulo `p`. For all of them, `p` divides `n+1`,
and hence divides the central binomial coefficient.
