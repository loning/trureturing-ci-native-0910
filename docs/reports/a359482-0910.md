## Sequence totality preregistration

Before implementing the greedy sequence, register its required totality
argument: put P=10^(length D(x)), R(0)=x, R(k+1)=x+P*R(k). The decimal word
of R(k) repeats D(x), so y=P*R(k) is a legal successor: x+y has k+2 copies
of D(x), and the concatenation has those copies followed by length D(x)
zeros. These successors are unbounded, hence one avoids every finite history.
This is an additional necessary construction, not an assumption of no small
digits. Then take the natural infimum of positive unused legal successors.
